⚠ Critical Warning

Applying These Laws Inside an Organization

These laws frequently conflict with corporate ego, conventional wisdom, and internal politics. Knowing them is the easy part.

The Perception Problem

The Law of Perception runs counter to cultures where "being better" through benchmarking is deeply ingrained. Management rarely welcomes challenges to the better-product strategy.

The Focus Resistance

Organizations instinctively resist narrowing focus. Offices full of bright people feel compelled to expand. Proposing pruning is extremely difficult to sell to leadership who equate bigger with better.

The Sacrifice Paradox

Offering everything to everybody is deeply wired into most companies. Nobody wants to give up any customer segment or product line — even when doing so would make them dramatically stronger.

The Line Extension Trap

Management believes successful brands have equity that can be stretched to anything. You won't find one board director in twelve willing to challenge this "basic truth."

The bottom line: If you violate the laws, you risk failure in the marketplace. If you apply them, you risk being ignored within your organization. Have patience — management changes, but the laws of marketing do not. Success is the best revenge.
