The Core Idea

Successful people aren't necessarily smarter, better educated, or better-looking. What separates them is their mastery of human interaction — a learnable skill. Leil Lowndes spent years studying top communicators, analyzing their body language, word choices, timing, and social instincts, then broke each insight into a concrete, nameable technique anyone can practice.

"Your body is a twenty-four-hour broadcasting station revealing to anyone within eyeshot precisely how you feel at any given moment. Every smile, every frown, every syllable can draw others toward you or make them want to run away."
