A symbol is not a sign or a decoration. It is a threshold — an image that gathers more meaning than any single explanation can hold. To dwell with a symbol is to allow the seen to point toward the unseen, the particular toward the universal.

What follows is a curated wandering through some of the great archetypal images that have appeared again and again across cultures and centuries — in myth, in dream, in art, in the quiet attentiveness of poets and the careful labor of alchemists.
