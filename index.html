The Three Traffic Temperatures

Not All Traffic Is Created Equal

Understanding the three types of traffic — and which one to build first — is the difference between a company that survives every Google slap and one that disappears overnight.

01
Traffic You Earn

Work Your Way In

Free, organic traffic you earn through relationships, content, and being a guest on other people's platforms. Harder to scale, but converts at the highest rate of all traffic types.

  • Podcast interviews & guest posts
  • Dream 100 collaborations

Buy Your Way In

  • Podcast sponsorships

The Conversion Framework

Hook. Story. Offer.

Natalie Hodson used this exact framework to sell 120,000 copies of one e-book. Every ad that converts, every email that gets opened, every video that drives sales — all follow this one timeless pattern.

1
🎣

HOOK

The pattern interrupt that stops the scroll. Every picture, headline, subject line, and video thumbnail is a hook. The better your hooks, the more attention you command — and attention is the currency of the internet.

Example: An image of a fitness mom who accidentally peed her pants during a workout. Impossible to scroll past. It stopped Jessica cold — and sold 120,000 e-books.
2
📖

STORY

Stories build two things: desire for your offer, and connection to you as the Attractive Character. People buy emotionally and justify logically — your story triggers the emotion. Even if they don't buy today, they become your follower.

Example: Natalie told the embarrassing story, introduced the doctor, showed the solution, and built a relationship with every mom who felt seen. The story created desire before any offer was made.
3
💸

OFFER

The offer doesn't always mean selling something. It could be "like this post," "subscribe," or "click the link." But make it irresistible. If people aren't taking action, it's almost always the offer — increase the value until they can't say no.

Example: The e-book + bonuses (diet tips, exercises, training programs) for just $47. The offer was so well-matched to the pain in the story that Jessica literally ran to find her credit card.

🔑 The Golden Diagnostic

If your ad isn't converting, if your funnel isn't selling, if your emails aren't opening — it's always the Hook, the Story, or the Offer. Fix one of those three things and watch everything change.

📸 Instagram Traffic Secrets

Instagram is the most powerful platform for building an Attractive Character relationship with your audience. Stories create intimacy. The JK5 method builds brand. And the Highlights mini-webinar hack converts followers into buyers 24/7.

  • →JK5 Method: Pick 5 brand categories, rotate posts between them. Build connection beyond what you sell.
  • →Profile hooks: Every image is a hook. Run every post through the ABCDQ test before publishing.
  • →Stories = Reality Show: Post 10-30 Stories per day documenting your journey in 15-second clips.
  • →Highlights Webinar Hack: Create a 15-50 Story "mini webinar" once per month using the Perfect Webinar script. Save as a Highlight — it sells forever.
  • →Collabs with Dream 100: Q&A swaps that expose you to new audiences and grow both channels simultaneously.
  • →Paid shout-outs: Pay Dream 100 members to post about you with tags back to your profile.

👥 Facebook Traffic Secrets

Facebook is the world's biggest party. Your personal profile is your home. Your Fan Page is your show. Groups are your hangout. Messenger is your private distribution channel. Each serves a different — essential — role.

  • →Personal Profile = Home: Make daily posts rotating through your JK5 categories. Post links in comments (not posts) to avoid algorithmic penalty.
  • →Fan Page = Your Show: Only post content you'd pay $10-20 to boost. Produced value videos, Live value videos, and Live Perfect Webinars.
  • →Groups = Hangout: Run a community group. Provides free organic reach, builds tribe loyalty, and generates hundreds of new customers per month.
  • →Messenger = Distribution: Never blast promotions. Send conversations once/week: "Are you still looking for X?" Then give them a choice. Highest open rates available.
  • →Network in groups: Find groups with your dream customers, spend 30 min/day answering questions, providing value with zero pitching.

🔍 Google Traffic Secrets

Google SEO has evolved through Panda, Penguin, Hummingbird, and Fred — each update punishing spammers and rewarding those who align with Google's core goal: the best user experience. Give Google what it wants and it sends unlimited traffic.

  • →Find Dream 100 Keywords: Build your dream keyword list, then find 9 long-tail variations for each. Total: 100 keywords to dominate.
  • →Skyscraper Technique: Find content ranked #1 for your keyword, create a longer, better, more comprehensive version. Get all their backlinks pointing to you instead.
  • →Linkable Asset (Letterman Lists): "21 Best Tools for X" or "57 Ways to Y" — content people naturally link to and Google loves to rank.
  • →Guest Posting: 1 guest post/week on Dream 100 blogs. Get instant traffic from their readers AND quality backlinks for your skyscraper articles.
  • →Buy on ranked pages: While waiting to rank organically, buy banner ads on pages already ranking for your dream keywords. Get their qualified traffic now.

YouTube is the only platform where your content compounds over time. A video posted 6 years ago can still drive 10,000+ views per month today. Russell's affiliate Joe Marfoglio posted two videos that generated 2 million total views — years after upload.

  • →Channel Focus Phrase: Build your entire channel around one root keyword like "how to shave" — then create 100+ videos with that root + variations.
  • →ABC Keyword Hack: Type your root keyword + letter A in YouTube search, collect suggestions. Repeat with B, C... = 500+ video ideas instantly.
  • →Discoverable Videos: Hook (15 sec) → Trailer (5 sec) → Intro (15-30 sec) → Content (7-12 min) → CTA. Optimized to rank for keywords and convert viewers to subscribers.
  • →Binge-Watch Playlists: Break long content into 5-10 min episodes in a playlist. Each video auto-plays the next, sending binge signals to YouTube's algorithm that boost your entire channel.
  • →Buy on Dream 100 videos: Place pre-roll ads on any YouTube video or channel in your Dream 100 — directly in front of your ideal audience.

The Step-by-Step Traffic Roadmap

Here's exactly what to do, in what order. Don't skip steps. Don't try to do everything at once. Follow this sequence and you'll build a traffic foundation that no Google slap or Zanos snap can destroy.

🎯

Step 1 — Define Your Dream Customer Avatar

Write out 12+ phrases your customers say when moving away from pain. Write 12+ phrases they say when moving toward pleasure. These phrases reveal where they congregate and what hooks will grab them.

Time: 2-3 hours. This is the foundation everything else is built on. Do not skip this.
📋

Step 2 — Build Your Dream 100 List

Pick one platform. Find 100+ influencers, podcasters, bloggers, and group owners who have already congregated your dream customers. Map both interest-based and search-based congregations.

Time: 3-4 hours. If Russell were your $100K consultant, this is what he'd have you do first. It's that important.
🌱

Step 3 — Start Digging Your Well

Subscribe to their content. Comment daily on their posts. Buy their products. Message them personally. Provide value before asking for anything. Start this process 30-60 days before you need them to promote for you.

Time: 15 minutes, twice per day. This builds relationships worth millions over time.
🎙️

Step 4 — Launch Your Show & Publish Daily

Pick one channel (podcast, YouTube, blog, or social). Commit to publishing daily for 12 months minimum. Document your journey — don't create from scratch. Your first 45 episodes won't be good, and that's perfect — because no one is watching yet.

Key truth: If you publish every day for a year, you'll never have to worry about money again.
💰

Step 5 — Build a Break-Even Funnel

Create a front-end offer with an order form bump + 2 OTOs. The goal is to get your average cart value to equal or exceed your cost per click. When you achieve this, you have an unlimited advertising budget.

Once break-even is achieved, scale spend as fast as possible. Spend $1, get $1 back, grow your list for free.
📧

Step 6 — Build Your Follow-Up Funnel

Write Soap Opera Sequences for each product in your value ladder. Use Emotion (days 1-3) → Logic (days 4-5) → Fear/Urgency (days 6-7). Add retargeting ads to reach people who opened emails but didn't click. Multiply your profits 16x.

This is where 80% of your revenue is hiding. Every lead you've ever generated is money waiting to be unlocked.
🚀

Step 7 — Scale with Your Affiliate Army

Turn your Dream 100 into affiliates. Offer 40-50%+ commission. Create an affiliate center with done-for-you email swipes, banner ads, and social posts. Pay them as much as possible — they'll promote you over competitors who pay less.

One good affiliate who says yes can bring in more customers than 6 months of your own paid ads.
