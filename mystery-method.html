Chapter One

The Mystery Behind Casanova

Born in Venice in 1725, Giacomo Casanova documented 122 liaisons across his 73-year life — not with anyone, but with the high-value women of European aristocracy. His selectivity is the point. Mystery uses Casanova — not the fictional Don Juan, who "screwed anything that moved" — as the role model precisely because he was picky.

The Model
Casanova

122 high-value women. Selective. Quality over quantity. His autobiography remains wildly popular 200 years later.

The Counter-Model
Don Juan

Fictional. Thousands of women indiscriminately. The literary equivalent of pure quantity — the opposite of what Mystery teaches.

The Seven-Hour Discovery

By backward-engineering his own successes — "building" a girlfriend and then asking how did I do that? — Mystery identified that courtship from meeting to sex consistently took four to ten hours, averaging seven. This became the foundational Seven-Hour Rule.

🎯
The Counterintuitive Secret To get the attention of a beautiful woman, you must first disqualify yourself as a potential suitor. If you approach telegraphing interest, she immediately knows you find her high-value — which makes you lower-value by comparison. The solution: demonstrate value before revealing interest.

Phase A1

Open — Crossing the Social Hook Point

A1 begins when you open a set and ends when the set wants you to stay. That moment of social acceptance is the hook point. Before it, you're an uninvited stranger. After it, you're a welcomed guest.

🎯
The Three-Second Rule Within three seconds of spotting a woman you're attracted to, open her set. Waiting gives approach anxiety time to compound. Moving quickly also reads as natural confidence — she can't tell you were nervous because you didn't hesitate.

Indirect vs. Direct Openers

Direct Opener

"Wow, you are beautiful; my name is Glen." Conveys confidence but immediately telegraphs interest, alienates her friends, and triggers her protection shield before you've demonstrated any value.

Rarely Recommended

Indirect Opener

A story, observation, or question that opens conversation without telegraphing sexual interest. Lets you assess group dynamics, demonstrate personality, and earn the right to be there before revealing intent.

MM Standard

Protection Shield

Beautiful women have developed strategies — individually and collectively — to screen out low-value approaches:

✗Wearing a ring
✗"I have a boyfriend"
✗Surrounding with friends
✗Sitting in hard-to-approach spots
✗Being "bitchy"
✗Negative body language
✗No eye contact
✗Lack of responsiveness

These strategies often aren't who she really is — they're learned responses to constant unwanted approaches. An indirect opener that doesn't telegraph interest is the key to bypassing the shield entirely.

False Time Constraints (FTCs)

When you open, you haven't demonstrated value yet. Her subconscious asks: How long is this guy going to be here? A false time constraint preempts that discomfort by creating the impression that you're leaving anyway.

"I can only stay a sec — I'm here with friends, but check this out..."

"I've only got a minute, so I've got to tell you about..."

Once you reach the hook point, use another FTC. The set now wants you to stay — announcing you're leaving creates fear of loss and begins the cycle of baiting the target to chase.

Body Language Fundamentals

Lean Back
The person leaning back holds more power. Lean in = you value her more than she values you. Lean back = equal or higher value. Never "peck" (lean in every time you speak).
Body Angle on Open
When running your opener, don't face the set with your full body. Head turned toward them, body angled away — as if you're just passing through. As they hook, gradually turn to face them. Reward their engagement.
Body Rocking
A physical false time constraint. Shift weight to your back leg mid-sentence as if about to leave, then come back as you continue talking. Nonverbal "I'm leaving... but actually..." loop.
Voice Tonality
Deep, from the diaphragm. Speak slowly with pauses. Speak loudly enough to project confidence. Eliminate "uh," "like," and "you know." Expressiveness in tone matters more than content.

Social Proof

Be the most sociable person in the room. Move from set to set — not scanning predatorily but talking genuinely with everyone. As you do, adjacent sets notice. You appear to have friends everywhere. Women see this and their preselection switches activate before you even approach them.

♟️
Canned Material Field-tested routines that generate consistent responses. The delivery matters more than the content. A comedian performing a joke for the 50th time has ironed out every timing beat — pickup artists do the same with social routines. Incongruence (the words don't match the delivery) is fatal. Naturalization takes ~24 repetitions.

Group Theory — Lead the Men, the Women Follow

Beautiful women are rarely alone. They cluster with trusted friends in groups. The traditional approach — wait for her to be alone — drastically limits options and accelerates nothing. Mystery Method approaches the group.

Single Set

Approach directly. No eye contact wait — just walk up, make eye contact, smile. Don't establish eye contact then stall — that breaks the Three-Second Rule and signals chickening out.

Two-Set

Target + one friend (obstacle). Win the obstacle first. Neg the target while talking to the obstacle. Once obstacle likes you, pay attention to target. Wing may then occupy obstacle to enable isolation.

Three-Set and More

Target + two+ obstacles. Same process — disarm all obstacles before IOI-ing target. In a three-set you can isolate the target because her friends still have each other. Easier than the two-set.

Mixed Set

Includes men. Initiate with the men first. Befriend them. Ask the waypoint question. If a man is the boyfriend — lucky you befriended him before expressing interest in his girlfriend.

Multiple Conversational Threads

Familiar friends naturally jump between topics — they don't exhaust one thread then move to the next. New acquaintances run one thread to completion and then feel awkward silence. By running multiple threads simultaneously, you create the feeling of deep familiarity even with someone you just met.

Thread Cutting
When a thread becomes boring, sad, or otherwise unhelpful — cut it and introduce a new one immediately. Don't wait for her to start a bad thread. Lead. Her boredom in a conversation is your responsibility.

Winging — The Wing Rules

  1. He who opens the set leads the set. Gets first choice of target.
  2. Your primary purpose as wing: help the pickup artist get his target.
  3. Never steal your wing's target (even if he's temporarily ignoring or negging her).
  4. Occupy obstacles so the player gets more one-on-one time.
  5. Always agree with your wing. Never take the girls' side over his.
  6. Introduce your wing with an accomplishment intro, not just a name.
  7. Never leave your wing standing unacknowledged — disrespecting him drops your value.

Locking In

For as long as you're standing in front of a seated or bar-leaning group, they hold the power position — better sightlines, more comfort. The goal is to take their spot.

The Spin Maneuver: Say "go like this" (hold out hand → compliance test). Take her hand when she mirrors you. Say "do a little spin" (second compliance test). Spin her, and step into her bar position. You're now leaning back comfortably; she's facing you with her back to the room. The room sees her up on you, not the reverse.

Chapter Eight

Conversation & Comfort — C1

"The Game is played in comfort. Everything else was merely to get to this point." — Mystery

All the attraction and investment built so far is useless without genuine comfort. A woman who enjoyed your company at the club but doesn't know you won't return your calls. Comfort solves this.

What Comfort Builds

  • Wide conversational rapport
  • Sense of comfort and trust
  • Feeling that the interaction is real and genuine
  • A felt connection

Why Numbers Flake

The primary cause: not enough comfort. She had fun but doesn't know you. She's not sure how you'll behave when you're alone. She's not uncomfortable enough to say no to seeing you but not comfortable enough to say yes.

The Freeze-Out

When she behaves badly in the comfort phase, you can't use attraction-phase tools (negging, ball-busting). Instead: withdraw attention. She'll ask "What's wrong?" You say "Nothing" — not sulking, genuinely unaffected, a slight loss of interest. She feels the loss and course-corrects.

After the Freeze-Out
Immediately give her an easy compliance test so you can reward her for passing it. Punishment alone creates fear of you. Punishment → easy win → reward creates a trained association: behave well = feel good.

Commonality

People are more attracted to those they consider similar to themselves. Draw out information about her and demonstrate shared experiences, values, or worldview. Serendipity — the feeling that this was meant to be — is a powerful comfort-builder.

Caveat: Visibly fishing for commonalities reads as desperate. Use conversation games (music game, question game) to create organic, surprise moments of connection.

Conspiracy

Once you've bounced together, done A3, and started building comfort — a shared frame emerges naturally. Inside jokes. Nicknames. The two of you "in on something" together. This is conspiracy. Nurture it; it's what distinguishes you from "some guy she met at a bar."

Vulnerability

Sharing vulnerabilities creates emotional connection. Too early = try-hard. At the right time in comfort = powerful. Structure stories where vulnerability appears accidentally revealed. Her self-disclosure follows yours — the question game is excellent for this.

Jealousy Plotlines

For a woman, jealousy is often a more powerful motivator than horniness. The moment she experiences jealousy is frequently the first moment she consciously realizes how attracted she is to you.

How to Create Jealousy

  • Bring a pivot (female friend) who sits on your lap at the right moment
  • Backward-merge to a previous set — introduce her to a previous target
  • Forward-merge — open a new set and bring your target with you
  • On the phone: "Hold on, I have another call" (3–4 times)
  • Casually mention "a girlfriend of mine..." in conversation

Grounding

When she asks "What do you do?" — the answer alone creates distance. The grounding routine gives her your backstory so she can follow your journey and feel she understands you.

"When I was young..."
Childhood dreams and ambitions. She sees you as a regular kid — commonality established.
"When I was a teenager..."
The journey — what happened, how you got from there to here. Stories, setbacks, turning points.
"Now I'm a [X]..."
Where you are today and what's next. She's been on this journey with you — the distance is gone.

Like a road trip where the conversation makes the miles disappear, skilled comfort-building makes the courtship advance seamlessly. She's suddenly at your place without having "decided" to go.

Get her number before bouncing. That way if the bounce fails, you haven't settled — you just happened to get her number along the way.

Time Bridge

Used when you can't bounce that night. Not just exchanging numbers — you leave with a specific plan: "I'm going to the Magic Castle Thursday. I'd like you to come." A number without a plan is a stairway to nowhere. A time bridge has two firm sides.

Phone Game

  • Never let any single number become too important — practice with all of them
  • Call the same night after leaving — short, casual chat, then let her go
  • Call a day or two later to chat, not necessarily to set a date
  • Invite her to fun activities the way you'd invite any friend
  • Time on the phone counts toward the seven hours
  • Numbers flake — keep rotating, keep practicing; as game improves, flake rate drops

Dating — A Repeatable Structure

If you're getting numbers but not sex, design a specific date structure you can repeat and refine. Build in venue changes, hours together, and a natural path back to your place.

Sample Date Routine (Mystery's)

7:30 PM — She arrives at your place
You "forgot" something — run back upstairs. She sees your apartment briefly, making it familiar (not unknown/scary) for later.
8:00 PM — Sushi
Comfort building, question game, palm reading, vibing. Various restaurant rituals as shared experience.
9:00 PM — Live music venue nearby
Another bounce. VIP card means no line. More drinks, push-pull to maintain tension.
10:00 PM — Concert room
Leading through crowd = natural physical closeness. Two hours of shared laughter.
Midnight — Back to your place
"You can come up, but only for a few minutes — I have to work tomorrow." Scarcity. Then balcony, then bedroom.

Last-Minute Resistance (LMR)

LMR is approach anxiety's counterpart — a hardwired fear response right before first-time sex. It's normal, not a rejection. Her emotional circuitry is protecting against genetic investment in a man who might not stick around.

Causes of LMR

  • Insufficient comfort built
  • She doesn't feel she's "won" you over
  • She hasn't earned your interest in her eyes
  • ASD activated by premature escalation
  • She's worried about how you'll act after

Responses to LMR

  • Agree with her token resistance — don't argue
  • "I know, baby. We should stop." (Keep going.)
  • Freeze-out: turn on lights, check email, make a sandwich
  • Play solid game earlier so LMR barely arises
  • Convey pair-bond throughout C phases: "You're on my mind more than I expected"
⚖️
Solid Game Reduces LMR The checklist: Are you healthy and socially comfortable? Do you convey non-neediness? Have you demonstrated preselection? Has she invested and chased? Have you built comfort over 7 hours and multiple venues? Did you use a jealousy plotline? If yes to all — LMR is rarely an issue.

Mystery's Kiss Gambit

"Shh! You talk a lot. Would you like to kiss me?"

  • She says yes: Kiss her.
  • She says maybe / why / I dunno: "Let's find out" — advance 90%, let her close the 10%.
  • She says no: "I didn't say you could — it just looked like you had something on your mind." (Don't ask why.)
  • She says not yet / not here: "I understand." — Logistical issue, not rejection. Kiss her later in private.
